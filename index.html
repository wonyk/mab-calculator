How this forecast works

The forecast uses your known MAB through the effective date, then balances after your custom withdrawal on the transaction date and any planned transfers. Base interest is predicted for the entire month, including completed days.

Already credited interest belongs in your current balance. The optional OCBC bonus is last month's bonus expected to post later this month; it is an entered credit, not earnings from your proposed withdrawal. Do not also enter it as a planned transfer.

Assumes base interest posts on the final day and counts in that day's closing balance. The year-day basis is an estimate; interest is truncated to cents. Do not also add this month's base-interest credit as a planned deposit. The custom withdrawal replaces the recommended move for its forecast; it is not an extra withdrawal.
